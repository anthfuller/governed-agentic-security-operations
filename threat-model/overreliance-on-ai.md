# Overreliance on AI

Analysts may accept fluent output as fact, skip contrary evidence, or let recommendations become de facto decisions.

## Controls

- label generated content and expose source references, uncertainty, and limitations;
- require explicit support status for material claims;
- distinguish evidence, derived facts, hypotheses, and recommendations;
- give reviewers a meaningful reject and request-more-evidence path;
- sample accepted and rejected outputs for quality review;
- prohibit model or judge output from granting authorization;
- use external policy and tool enforcement even when confidence is high.

Unsupported claims, unresolved scope, missing evidence, or reviewer inability to assess the material blocks consequential use. A quality score cannot compensate for missing authority or scope.

Assurance metrics should include unsupported-claim rate, reviewer disagreement, scope failures, reversals, and downstream corrections; do not rely on aggregate model confidence alone.

Related controls: `GASO-GOV-001`, `GASO-EVD-003`, `GASO-ASR-001`, `GASO-ASR-002`.

# Assurance Result Template

Use an assurance result to record evidence-support, scope, unsupported-claim, or output-quality checks. The artifact must state its limitations and must set `authoritative` to `false`.

An assurance component may recommend pass, fail, or review. It cannot approve an action, issue a policy decision, expand scope, or replace accountable human judgment.

Validate [`assurance-result.yaml`](assurance-result.yaml) with `gaso validate`.
